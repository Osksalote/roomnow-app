NamStay

Test version

Find a free room in any town in Namibia. Owners and receptionists manage their place in the same app.

Android

Download the Android app (.apk)
  1. Open the downloaded file.
  2. If asked, allow "install from this source".
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

iPhone

Open NamStay in Safari
  1. Open the link above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. NamStay now opens from its own icon, like an app.
